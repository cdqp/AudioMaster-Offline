CDQP Offline Audio Master
CDQP Offline Audio Master is a powerful, 100% local, and free audio processing suite that runs entirely in your web browser. Your audio files never leave your device, ensuring maximum privacy and zero latency from server uploads.

Features
Mastering: Finalize your music with genre-specific presets, focusing on clarity, dynamics, and stereo imaging.
Music Repair: Fix AI-generated audio (e.g., Suno), remove noise, and clean up "muddy" or harsh mixes.
Voice Repair: Specialized tools to clean and enhance spoken, sung, or poorly recorded vocal tracks.
Stem Extraction: High-quality local spectral/phase separation to isolate vocals from instrumentals.
MIDI Generation: Instantly generate random melodies (4 to 16 notes) and export them as standard .mid files.
Privacy First
This application is designed with a "local-only" architecture. No audio files are sent to a server; all DSP (Digital Signal Processing) happens in your browser.

How to use
Drop: Simply drag and drop your audio or video file into the interface.
Choose: Select the module that fits your needs (Mastering, Repair, or Extraction).
Download: Process your audio locally and save the result instantly.
Technical Details
Built with high-performance Web Audio API/DSP.
Multi-language support (30+ languages).
Responsive UI designed for both desktop and mobile use.
